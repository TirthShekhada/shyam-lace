Journey behind the
Shyam Lace

SINCE

Founded in 2012 in the bustling textile capital of Surat, Gujarat, SHYAM LACE began as a modest venture with a grand vision: to define the standards of fashion accessories and lace manufacturing in India and beyond.

Each Lace carries its own signature.

VARIETY

The company offers a diverse range of products, including Poshak Lace, Embroidery Lace, Net Embroidery Lace, Gamthi Lace, Zalar Lace, Gota Cutwork Lace, Jarkan Lace, Sequence Lace, Latkan Lace, Gold by Gold Lace, Handwork Lace, Beads Lace, Blouse Dori, Slogan Lace, Khess, Assan, and Door Toran.

Each lace represents its own identity.
